BEHIND THE NONSENSE

WHO MAKES THIS STUFF?

OuttaPocketTV is a comedy-first creative project: original characters, unpredictable sketches, satire and ideas that refuse to behave.

MORE THAN ONE JOKE. MORE THAN ONE CHARACTER.

OuttaPocketTV is a growing collection of short-form comedy scenes, animated characters and visual experiments. The idea is simple: a ridiculous premise deserves to be produced like it matters. Some jokes land in seconds. Others need a recurring cast, a little world-building or a punchline that arrives from the wrong direction.

The channel moves between animated satire, awkward everyday situations, absurd technology, and exaggerated reactions. The format changes with the idea instead of forcing every character into one familiar template. You can watch the finished productions on our video gallery, with direct links back to the original TikTok uploads.

MEET THE RECURRING TROUBLEMAKERS

POP-POP & GARY

Pop-Pop brings an older generation's confidence into thoroughly modern nonsense. Gary plays across from him in an animated corner of the universe where one conversation can drift into a whole new problem.

JAHZI

A recurring voice for bold opinions, confrontations and everyday absurdity. Jahzi's scenes work by pushing an ordinary conversation well past where it should have ended.

DYSKLOSIA

Exhausted expressions, sharp cuts and questionable judgment are part of Dysklosia's brand of comedy. Her short reactions and Halloween chaos belong to one particularly unpredictable part of the cast.

BLACKGPT

A fictional technology-themed character and series that plays with what happens when people ask a smart home assistant to handle very human situations. The technology is the setup; the joke is what happens next.

WHY WE MAKE SHORT COMEDY

A short video has no room to waste time. The opening image, the first line and the reaction can do the work of a much longer scene. That constraint is part of the fun: a story can fit into fifteen seconds, or a recurring character can come back with a completely different disaster next week.

OuttaPocketTV uses digital animation and AI-assisted production tools as part of its creative workflow, alongside original concepts, editing, character decisions and comedic direction. The characters and production choices are curated rather than presented as an automated feed of unrelated clips.
