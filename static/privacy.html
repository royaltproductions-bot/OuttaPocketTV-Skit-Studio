WEBSITE POLICIES

PRIVACY POLICY.

What information the site uses, where it goes, and what choices visitors have.

Effective date: October 30, 2026. This policy applies to the OuttaPocketTV website and its Skit Studio. It describes the information used to operate the site, to provide a requested preview or quote, and to process orders.

WHAT YOU CHOOSE TO SEND

When you request a free concept preview or a custom video, you provide an email address and an original idea. You may also provide creative style preferences, instructions, and a requested video length. A custom-length quote request contains the information entered into that form. We use these details to respond to the request, create the requested preview, arrange an order, and provide customer support.

The free sample is limited to one per email address. We maintain the sample-use status in Stripe customer metadata so the limit can be checked after the website restarts. Paid orders and custom-quote requests are stored in Stripe records, including the selected package and creative directions.

AI PREVIEWS AND VIDEO PRODUCTION

We send the submitted scene idea and selected style to OpenAI to generate a concept image. The site uses a pseudonymous safety identifier for this request rather than sending your email address in the image-generation request. OuttaPocketTV adds its preview branding before returning the image to your browser.

Free preview images are not saved in a permanent gallery on our web server. Your browser may keep a temporary session copy so you can revisit the generated preview during the current session. This website does not automatically generate a final paid video: completed MP4s are produced and delivered manually through customer correspondence.

PAYMENTS AND REQUEST RECORDS

Stripe handles secure checkout and the associated payment information. OuttaPocketTV does not receive your full payment card number from Stripe. Stripe stores the checkout session and transaction records used to verify purchase and delivery status. Information held by Stripe is also subject to Stripe's privacy policy.

TIKTOK VIDEOS AND THIRD-PARTY MEDIA

Our entertainment gallery includes videos embedded from TikTok. When your browser loads these players, TikTok may receive technical details such as IP address, device/browser information, and interaction information and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy terms. You can use each video's direct TikTok link instead of playing it here.

GOOGLE ADS AND COOKIES

Our homepage includes Google AdSense site-verification code. If Google approves advertising on this site, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers for advertising, including ads based on visits to this and other websites. Third-party advertising providers, including Google, use cookies to deliver and measure ads. Google advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to our pages and other sites.

You can control Google's personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. You can also learn about options for certain third-party advertisers at aboutads.info. Your browser also provides cookie controls. Where required by applicable law, we must obtain or manage the appropriate consent before serving relevant advertising cookies.

SECURITY, LOGS AND RETENTION

Render hosts the website. Basic technical data, including IP addresses, may be processed during requests and temporarily used to rate-limit repeated free-preview attempts. We do not run a permanent local customer database on the web server. Stripe may retain purchase, customer and quote records according to its systems and applicable obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect access, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

YOUR CHOICES AND QUESTIONS

You can choose not to use the interactive forms and can watch original posts on TikTok directly. To ask about your submitted information, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern, email outtapockettv101@gmail.com. We will review requests subject to legal and payment-record requirements. Relevant changes to this policy will be posted here.
