THE OUTTAPOCKETTV UNIVERSE

MEET THE CAST.

The fun of a recurring cast is that every new setting changes the joke. An old man, an opinionated young voice, an overconfident assistant or a woman who looks like she needs a week off can all end up in a completely different kind of sketch.

01 / ANIMATED MISCHIEF

POP-POP & GARY

Pop-Pop brings veteran confidence to an animated world that has no intention of respecting his age. Gary is part of the same universe, trading reactions and unexpected revelations as the situation gets stranger.

02 / THE HOT TAKE

JAHZI

Jahzi is built for the conversation that should have ended three lines ago. His scenes use quick exchanges and over-the-top convictions to turn an ordinary topic into a larger comic argument.

03 / TECHNOLOGY GONE SIDEWAYS

BLACKGPT

What happens when a fictional smart assistant enters the mess of everyday life? The BlackGPT sketches turn familiar technology into a character with a very different idea of what “helpful” means.

04 / EXHAUSTED ENERGY

DYSKLOSIA

Dysklosia’s reactions are often the punchline. Sharp cuts, tired looks and terrible decisions push a simple moment into absurd comedy—from mental-health memes to her Halloween misadventure.

WHY THE JOKES LOOK DIFFERENT

Some stories work as cartoons, some as realistic-looking performances, and some as five-second visual reactions. OuttaPocketTV mixes original writing, character direction, editing and AI-assisted production to serve the joke rather than one fixed visual style.
